Half of the stimuli are frequent 5-letter English words (using only words whose 5 letters are all different), and the other half are pseudowords obtained be transposing  two
consecutive letters anywhere in the word.

Each trial begins with a fixation cross, followed by a 5-letter stimulus (word or pseudoword) presented for 200 ms. The presentation is divided into four 50 ms frames. In each frame, all five letters are displayed simultaneously, but the visibility of each letter is independently and randomly varied (e.g., high vs. low contrast). Thus, each trial contains 20 independently randomized visibility parameters: 5 letter positions × 4 time windows. Immediately after the final frame, a visual mask is presented, followed by a lexical decision response asking whether the presented string was a real word or not.
